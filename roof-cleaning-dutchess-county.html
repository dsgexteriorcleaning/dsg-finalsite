🏔️ Roof Soft Wash

Dutchess County roofs commonly develop black algae streaks and organic growth—especially in shaded areas. Our roof soft wash targets organic buildup using low pressure.

What We Remove:

  • Black streaks and algae discoloration
  • Moss and lichen (where present)
  • Organic buildup that can shorten shingle life

Our Safe Process:

  • Final inspection and next-step guidance

❓ Roof Cleaning FAQs

Is soft washing safe for asphalt shingles?

Yes—when done correctly, soft washing uses low pressure and a treatment designed to remove organic growth without high-pressure damage.

How fast will stains disappear?

Many roofs show improvement quickly, and some stains continue to lighten over time as the treatment keeps working and rain rinses the surface.
