Cortlandt Holiday Lighting FAQs

Do you serve both Cortlandt and Cortlandt Manor?

Yes. DSG evaluates residential and commercial holiday-lighting projects throughout Cortlandt, Cortlandt Manor and nearby northern Westchester communities.

Do you use nails or screws in the roof?

Our standard roofline installations use purpose-built lighting clips selected for the surface and display. We do not plan routine installations around penetrating roofing materials with nails or screws.

Are the lights included?

Yes. DSG’s managed seasonal service uses professional-grade lighting supplied as part of the approved display package.

When should I reserve an installation?

As early as possible. Preferred November windows are limited and are reserved as proposals are approved.

Is removal included?

Yes. Standard seasonal packages include scheduled post-holiday takedown and organized storage of the DSG display.
