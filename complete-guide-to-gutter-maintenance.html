Complete Guide to Gutter Maintenance

📅February 5th, 2025
⏱️10 min read
👤DSG Exterior Cleaning
[Featured Image: Clean vs Clogged Gutters Comparison]

Your gutters are one of the most underappreciated yet critical components of your home's defense system. They quietly channel thousands of gallons of water away from your foundation, siding, and landscaping every year. But when they fail? The damage can be catastrophic—and expensive.

Clogged gutters contribute to over $10 billion in property damage annually in the United States. From foundation cracks to basement flooding, from rotted fascia boards to landscape erosion, the consequences of neglected gutters compound over time.

This comprehensive guide will teach you everything you need to know about gutter maintenance: when to clean them, warning signs of problems, DIY vs professional cleaning, and how to prevent costly damage to your Hudson Valley home.
