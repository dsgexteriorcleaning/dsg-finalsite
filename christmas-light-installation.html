Holiday Lighting FAQ

Do I need to buy my own lights?

No. DSG's preferred program uses professional-grade materials selected for the design. That lets us maintain consistency and serviceability throughout the season.

Can you install lights I already own?

Customer-supplied lighting is not our core offering. We may decline installations where materials are incompatible, damaged or not appropriate for professional installation.

What happens if a section goes out?

For covered DSG-managed installations, contact us and we will schedule an in-season service visit according to the service terms in your proposal.

When should I reserve installation?

As early as possible. Prime November installation windows are limited and are assigned based on route, scope and approved proposal date.

Do you handle commercial properties?

Yes. DSG can quote selected commercial, restaurant, office, school and managed-property holiday lighting projects where the scope fits our installation capabilities.
