Holiday Lighting FAQs

Do I need to buy the lights?

No. DSG's managed service is built around professional-grade lighting supplied as part of your seasonal display package.

Is takedown included?

Yes. Standard DSG seasonal packages include scheduled post-season takedown.

What happens if a light goes out?

DSG-supplied lighting is covered by our in-season service for normal display issues. Damage caused by weather events, animals or third parties may require separate work.

Where do you install?

Primary residential service includes Dutchess, Putnam and Westchester Counties. Larger commercial opportunities may be evaluated outside the core residential service area.

When should I book?

Early. Holiday installation capacity is finite, and preferred November dates are reserved on a first-approved basis.
