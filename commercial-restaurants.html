The Exterior Problems Restaurants Can't Ignore

A dirty entrance costs you customers. A grimy dumpster pad costs you health code points. Here's what we see at restaurants across the Hudson Valley every week.

Grease-Stained Entrance & Sidewalk

High foot traffic plus kitchen ventilation creates sticky buildup on your entrance and sidewalk that regular mopping can't touch. First impressions matter.

Dumpster Pad Grease & Odor

Accumulated grease and food waste on dumpster pads attract pests, create odor, and are a direct health code concern during inspections.

Algae & Staining on Building Exterior

The north side of your building accumulates algae and mold over Hudson Valley winters. Customers see it on the way in — and it affects your image before they even walk through the door.

Outdoor Dining Area Buildup

Patio and outdoor seating surfaces collect organic growth, gum, food residue, and general grime that pressure washing removes completely.
